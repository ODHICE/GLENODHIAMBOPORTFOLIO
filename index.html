Featured Case Study

East Africans Study in Germany

Free Consultation Week — a campaign designed to drive awareness and encourage students to book free advice sessions at the Nairobi office.

24–29 August 2026

The objective.

The goal was simple: get more students to take the next step and book free consultation sessions at the Nairobi office.


Instead of relying on generic promotional messaging, I built the campaign around a real student concern — the frustration of having to pay just to get answers about studying abroad.

What I did.

  • Created a Reel built around the question: “Wait… you're paying just to ask questions?”
  • Launched the campaign with a bold “The Day Is Finally Here!” post.
  • Created a news-style “BREAKING NEWS” video ad.
  • Targeted new graduates with “You did it! Your next chapter starts now.”
  • Followed up with a “Free Application Support” Reel to move attention toward applications.
  • Repeated the office address and free-booking offer across campaign content.
  • Put paid distribution behind content that showed strong potential.
446K+ Total views across campaign content
2K+ Profile visits
124 New followers
224K+ Views on the biggest video

Content-level results.

“The Day Is Finally Here!” 45,304 views 28,667 reached 1,420 profile visits
“You're paying just to ask questions” 163,116 views 94,095 reached 45 new followers
“Breaking News” 13,839 views 13,078 reached 295 profile visits
“Free Application Support” 224,478 views 140,174 reached 66 new followers

Organic reach mattered.

Three of the four campaign pieces received the majority of their views organically, showing that the creative itself was capable of generating attention beyond paid distribution.


The “You're paying just to ask questions” Reel generated about 118,500 views organically, while the “Free Application Support” video generated about 154,500 organic views.

Paid media amplified it.

Paid distribution was used to put additional reach behind content and audiences with clear potential.


The consultation campaign generated 13,223 engagements and 939 link clicks from the paid push behind the “You're paying just to ask questions” Reel.


The “Free Application Support” promotion generated 17,555 engagements, 806 link clicks and 270 profile visits from its paid distribution.

What this campaign demonstrates

The strongest results came from connecting the creative to a real audience concern, testing different content angles and then using paid distribution to extend the reach of content that showed traction.
